# Licences

The images contain [FreeDOS](https://www.freedos.org/) 1.4. FreeDOS is a collection of programs, most of them under the GNU General
Public License (GPL); others are under other free licences (for example the LGPL, BSD, MIT, or public domain). Each installed package
holds its licence: in the images, look at `C:\FREEDOS\APPINFO\*.LSM` (the "Copying-policy" line of each package) and the documents in
`C:\FREEDOS\DOC`.
